    The quick briefing

    How to Play the Imposter Game

    Gather around one device and follow four simple steps.

    1. 01

      Set up your group

      Add 3–12 player names, choose one or more word categories, and optionally set a timer.

    2. 02

      Pass the phone

      Each player privately checks their role. Most see the same word; one player is the imposter.

    3. 03

      Give clues

      Take turns giving careful clues without saying the secret word. The imposter must improvise.

    4. 04

      Discuss and vote

      Compare clues and vote for the suspicious player. Catch the imposter before they fool the group.

    No prep required

    A social deduction game made for the room

    Imposter Game is a pass-the-phone party word game for friends, families, classmates, and coworkers. Everyone talks face to face, so the browser handles setup while your group creates the suspense. Start with familiar categories, keep clues short, and listen for the answer that sounds just a little too vague.

    Case notes

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is the imposter game?

    It is a social deduction word game. Most players know the same secret word, while one imposter tries to blend in without knowing it.

    How many players do we need?

    This version supports 3–12 players sharing one phone, tablet, or computer.

    Is it free, and do we need an account?

    It is free to play in the browser. There is no account, download, or installation.

    What gets saved?

    Player names, selected categories, and timer preferences are stored only in this browser. Secret roles and votes are never saved by the setup flow.

    What happens if the page refreshes?

    If a prepared round is interrupted, the site returns to a neutral restart screen. Your setup preferences remain available, but no secret role or vote state is restored.

    Can friends play remotely?

    Not in this local pass-the-phone version. Everyone should be together and share one device.
