      The quick briefing

      How to Play the Imposter Game

      This imposter word game turns one shared device into a quick social deduction game. Gather your group, keep each role private, and follow four simple steps to find the imposter.

      1. 01

        Set up your group

        Add 3–12 unique player names and choose one or more word categories. The optional discussion timer can be set to 1, 2, 3, or 5 minutes.

      2. 02

        Pass the phone

        Hand the device to each player in order. Most players privately see the same secret word. One player sees only that they are the imposter and must keep that role hidden.

      3. 03

        Give clues

        Take turns giving a short clue without saying the secret word. Innocent players show that they know the answer, while the imposter listens carefully and tries to blend in.

      4. 04

        Discuss and vote

        Compare clues, discuss who seems suspicious, and vote privately. If the imposter alone receives the most votes, the crew wins. An innocent plurality or any tie gives the imposter the win.

      No prep required

      A social deduction game made for the room

      Imposter Game is a local pass the phone game for friends, families, classmates, and coworkers. Everyone plays face to face while the browser handles secret roles, the optional timer, private voting, and results. After a round, choose Play Again to keep the same names and settings with a new word and imposter. New Setup lets you edit the group, while Restart asks for confirmation before returning to setup. No account or networked room is required.

      Case notes

      Frequently Asked Questions

      What is the imposter game?

      The imposter game is a party word game and social deduction game. Most players receive the same secret word, but one hidden imposter does not. Players give clues, discuss the answers, and try to identify who is bluffing.

      How do you play the imposter word game?

      Add your players, select categories, and start the round. Pass the device so everyone can reveal and hide a private role. Give one clue each without saying the secret word, discuss the clues, then pass the device again so every player can cast one private vote.

      How many players do you need?

      This version supports 3–12 players. Everyone should be in the same place and share one phone, tablet, or computer. Each player needs a unique name so private turns and vote totals remain clear.

      Is the game free, and does it require a download or account?

      Yes, the game is free to play in a modern browser. It does not require a download, installation, payment, or account. Once the static game files have loaded, the core round does not depend on a gameplay server.

      What does the imposter see?

      The imposter sees only that they are the imposter. They do not see the secret word or receive a hint. All other players see the same word and should hide it before passing the device.

      How do voting and ties work?

      Voting uses the same private pass-the-phone pattern as role reveals. Every player votes once and may vote for anyone, including themselves. Totals stay hidden until voting ends. The crew wins only when the imposter is the sole player with the highest total; an innocent plurality or any tie means the imposter wins.

      Can children or families play?

      Yes. The familiar word categories and simple clue format make it suitable for many family groups. Adults can choose categories appropriate for their players and help younger children understand that clues should describe the word without saying it directly.

      Does the game work on iPhone, Android, tablets, and computers?

      Yes. The responsive browser layout is designed for current iPhone and Android phones, tablets, laptops, and desktop computers. A phone is often easiest to pass around, but touch, mouse, and keyboard controls are supported.

      Are names or votes sent to a server?

      No. Gameplay is local to the device. Names, selected categories, and timer preferences may be saved in that browser for convenience. Secret roles and votes stay in temporary game memory and are not stored as round history or sent to a game server.

      Can friends play remotely?

      Not in this local-only version. It is designed as a face-to-face pass the phone game, so everyone should be together and use the same device. There are no online rooms, room codes, remote lobbies, or accounts.
